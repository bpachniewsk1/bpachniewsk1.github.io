Personal project · Anki add-on

Hanzi Grid

An Anki add-on I built for my own Chinese studies. It lays every character of the official HSK 3.1 list out as a grid and colours each one by how well I know it, with a red dot on the ones I keep forgetting. Other views group words by measure word and track grammar points level by level.

Watching the grid fill up turned out to be the best motivation I've found — and it shows exactly what to learn next.
